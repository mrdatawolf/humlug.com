HumLUG Events:

Lunch meetings:

HumLug meets for lunch and fun discussions about technology every friday at 11:30pm at Babe’s Pizza in Eureka.

Evening meetings:

We have monthly presentation focussed meetings on the third Thursday at 6:00PM at Babe’s Pizza in Eureka.

Next meeting: 6:00PM Mar 19th, 2026 at the Babe’s Pizza conference room

  • Brian - Report on his 1 week dive into Meshtastic and MeshCore ecosystems. He’ll be bringing a variety of devices for those who missed last Friday’s lunch. Some have screens and/or E-ink displays and keyboards and some have external antennas…

  • John - Trip report about the SCaLE conference in Pasadena and which sessions attended that might be of wider interest. The Youtube streams are at SCaLE Streams and keep an eye on the Videos tab too, because they may decide to chop up the 4-8 hour streams into individual session videos in the coming weeks. The SCaLE team of volunteers has done a good job of adding the time offset, name of session in that room at that time and linking to the expanded session description from the program in the description field of each Youtube video. So you can skip to the portion of the video stream that corresponds to the session you’re interested in.

  • Dave Davison - His latest enthusiasm, the Hyprland compositor/desktop environment for Wayland on his laptop.

  • Dave Rowe and Char - Progress made on the greenhouse automation project and their related local AI project.
